# Atlas community code of conduct

## Our standard

We want Atlas to be a technically rigorous, welcoming project. Participants should:

- discuss ideas and failures without attacking people;
- give specific, evidence-based technical feedback;
- respect different experience levels, identities, backgrounds, and accessibility needs;
- protect private user, customer, and security information;
- disclose conflicts of interest and relevant commercial affiliations; and
- accept maintainer decisions while remaining free to disagree constructively.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, credential/data disclosure, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope

This applies in project repositories, discussions, issue trackers, code review, community spaces, events, and public representation of the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainer through the contact method on the GitHub profile. For security matters use [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed in context. Responses may include a private warning, content removal, temporary participation limits, or a permanent ban.

Enforcement decisions should protect affected people, preserve relevant evidence, minimize unnecessary disclosure, and avoid retaliation.
